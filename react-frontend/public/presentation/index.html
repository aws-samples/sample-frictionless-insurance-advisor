The challenge

Why Insurance Advisors Need AI

Complexity & Information Overload

  • Insurance documents are complex, jargon-heavy, and difficult to interpret quickly
  • Advisors manage hundreds of products across multiple providers — impossible to know everything
  • Manual comparison is time-consuming and error-prone

Lost Momentum & Revenue

  • When customers ask questions, advisors often say "let me get back to you" and wait for the insurer — losing momentum and trust
  • Customers expect instant, personalized answers — not callbacks days later
  • Advisors miss cross-sell/upsell opportunities due to lack of holistic coverage visibility

Competitive pressure

Digital-first insurers and aggregators are raising the bar — advisors who can't deliver instant, informed answers lose deals to those who can.

Every day

Start the Day Knowing Who to Call

Today's priorities

The home screen ranks the customers who need attention, with every reason and one click to Draft email or Annual review. The rules run in code, so the list is the same every time

Practise a conversation

The voice assistant plays the customer from their record, friendly, neutral or sceptical, then scores the advisor on seven criteria and checks product claims against the sheets. Nothing is recorded or saved

One login per line

Life, General and Health advisors see their own customers, and the comparator and gap analysis switch by line. A customer with no Awesome policy is treated as a prospect

Gaps and forms from the profile

One click gives a coverage-gap analysis for Life, General, Health or all, or opens any of the ten application forms filled in with the customer's details

A form that keeps its answers

Opened from the profile or from the assistant, it is the same form. Answers typed or spoken stay put when it is closed or the advisor moves between pages

Test files for every customer

Download a sample third-party policy and upload it to see the document read into structured fields the advisor confirms before anything is saved

In every meeting

Ready Before the Customer Sits Down

Meeting prep in one click

A structured brief — customer snapshot, goals, discovery questions, products to discuss, objection handling, and a close — built from the profile, notes, other insurers' policies, promotions, and competitor material

Indicative pricing

Premium estimates for all ten products, calculated from rate tables in code and never by the model, with an affordability check against 10% of income. Always an estimate, never a quote

Notes that stay with the customer

Life events and interactions are kept on the customer's record. The assistant can add a note when asked, and never deletes one

Carrier strength at a glance

AM Best, S&P, and Moody's ratings sit beside every compared product, on screen and in the customer PDF

A competitor brochure on the spot

Upload a competitor's PDF brochure, compare it side by side with Awesome products, and let it delete itself within a day

Beyond the documents

General insurance questions get a live web search, with page titles and links shown. Awesome products, prices, and competitors never come from the web

Flagship capability

The Application Fills Itself

When the advisor and customer agree to start an application, the form opens beside the conversation and populates from it. In chat or by voice, the advisor keeps talking and fields land as they speak.

Ten Awesome products

Term, whole, universal, and variable life, critical illness, health, home, auto, travel, and disability — each with a published form of sections, fields, types, and mandatory flags

Answers carry over

All ten forms share the applicant details, and the four life forms share 18 of about 20 fields. Switch product mid-conversation and nobody is asked their date of birth twice

Confidence you can see

High-confidence answers fill green; anything less fills amber with a "Looks right" confirmation. Nothing is hidden from the advisor

Known details prefilled

Fields already on the customer record arrive filled and labelled as such. Precedence is manual > conversation > profile, so typed corrections are never overwritten

The schema never touches the model

Forms are read from S3 server-side and pushed straight to the browser. The model only receives the field paths — no wasted tokens, no invented fields

Guarded by design

A field write is rejected unless its path exists in the form actually open, and refused outright if no form is open. A printable PDF is one click away

Trust and control

Built to Be Trusted

Consent comes first

In voice, nothing is remembered, filled in, or transcribed until the advisor confirms the customer agreed. Declining ends the session and saves nothing

Transcripts that expire

Voice transcripts go to a private store, are readable only by the advisor who held the conversation, and expire after a day

Answers you can check

Sources are built from what the tools actually returned, so the AI cannot invent a citation

One shared guardrail

The same content, personal-data, and denied-topic policy (legal, investment, and tax advice) applies to chat, comparison, recommendations, and document reading. Voice runs under its system prompt and tool checks

Estimates, not promises

No binding quotes, approvals, or claim payouts. Prices are labelled indicative and calculated in code

The advisor stays in control

Policies and notes are saved only when the advisor asks or confirms, deletion stays in the app, and low-confidence document readings are checked field by field. Every tool call is tied to the signed-in advisor's own token, so one advisor's assistant can never act as another

Appendix

End-to-end on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore

Models: Claude Haiku 5.5 (chat, comparator, recommendations, document and brochure reading) · Nova 2.5 Sonic (voice). One shared Bedrock Guardrail — content filters, PII handling, denied topics and contextual grounding — gates the five Claude surfaces (voice is not covered). Before every tool call the gateway's request interceptor checks the advisor's own sign-in token, so a tool only ever acts as the signed-in advisor. Voice traces carry timings and tool names, not conversation content. Five CDK stacks; every AgentCore resource is stable CloudFormation.
