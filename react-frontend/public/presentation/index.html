Flagship capability

The Application Fills Itself

When the advisor and customer agree to start an application, the form takes over the panel and populates from the conversation. The advisor keeps talking; fields land as they speak.

Four life products

Term, whole, universal, and variable life — each with a published form schema of sections, fields, types, and mandatory flags

Answers carry over

All four schemas share the applicant, health, and beneficiary sections — 18 of ~20 fields. Switch product mid-conversation and nobody is asked their date of birth twice

Confidence you can see

High-confidence answers fill green; anything less fills amber with a "Looks right" confirmation. Nothing is hidden from the advisor

Known details prefilled

Fields already on the customer record arrive filled and labelled as such. Precedence is manual > voice > profile, so typed corrections are never overwritten

The schema never touches the model

Forms are read from S3 server-side and pushed straight to the browser. The model only receives the field paths — no wasted tokens, no invented fields

Guarded by design

A field write is rejected unless its path exists in the form actually open, and refused outright if no form is open

Appendix

End-to-end on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore

Models: Claude Haiku 4.5 (chat, comparator & recommendations) · Nova Sonic 2 (voice) · Claude Sonnet 5 (document extraction). One shared Bedrock Guardrail — content filters, PII handling, denied topics and contextual grounding — gates all five model surfaces. Five CDK stacks; every AgentCore resource is stable CloudFormation.
