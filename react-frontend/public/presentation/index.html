The challenge

Why Insurance Advisors Need AI

Complexity & Information Overload

  • Insurance documents are complex, jargon-heavy, and difficult to interpret quickly
  • Advisors manage hundreds of products across multiple providers — impossible to know everything
  • Manual comparison is time-consuming and error-prone

Lost Momentum & Revenue

  • When customers ask questions, advisors often say "let me get back to you" and wait for the insurer — losing momentum and trust
  • Customers expect instant, personalized answers — not callbacks days later
  • Advisors miss cross-sell/upsell opportunities due to lack of holistic coverage visibility

Competitive pressure

Digital-first insurers and aggregators are raising the bar — advisors who can't deliver instant, informed answers lose deals to those who can.

  • Insurance documents are long and full of jargon, and no advisor can know hundreds of products from many providers.
  • Comparing products by hand is slow and error-prone.
  • When customers ask a question, the answer waits for the insurer, and momentum and trust are lost. Cross-sell and upsell chances are missed too.
  • Digital-first insurers and aggregators raise the bar: advisors who cannot answer on the spot lose deals.

Flagship capability Part 2 of 2

The Application Fills Itself

Known details prefilled

Fields already on the customer record arrive filled and labelled as such. Precedence is manual > conversation > profile, so typed corrections are never overwritten

The schema never touches the model

Forms are read from S3 server-side and pushed straight to the browser. The model only receives the field paths — no wasted tokens, no invented fields

Guarded by design

A field write is rejected unless its path exists in the form actually open, and refused outright if no form is open. A printable PDF is one click away, and Send for Signature saves the finished application. Sending it again replaces that save instead of adding a duplicate

  • Details already on the customer record arrive prefilled and labelled. Manual entries win over conversation, and conversation wins over profile.
  • Forms are read from S3 on the server and pushed to the browser. The model only receives the field paths.
  • A field write is rejected unless its path exists in the form that is open.
  • A printable PDF is one click away. Send for Signature saves the finished application, and sending it again replaces that save.
Trust and control

Built to Be Trusted

Consent comes first

In voice, nothing is remembered, filled in, or transcribed until the advisor confirms the customer agreed. Declining ends the session and saves nothing

Transcripts that expire

Voice transcripts go to a private store, are readable only by the advisor who held the conversation, and expire after a day

Answers you can check

Sources are built from what the tools actually returned, so the AI cannot invent a citation

One shared guardrail

The same content, personal-data, and denied-topic policy (legal and tax advice) applies to chat, comparison, recommendations, and document reading. Voice runs under its system prompt and tool checks

Estimates, not promises

No binding quotes, approvals, or claim payouts. Prices are labelled indicative and calculated in code

The advisor stays in control

Policies and notes are saved only when the advisor asks or confirms, deletion stays in the app, and low-confidence document readings are checked field by field. Every tool call is tied to the signed-in advisor's own token, so one advisor's assistant can never act as another

  • Consent comes first in voice: nothing is remembered, filled in or transcribed until the advisor confirms the customer agreed.
  • Voice transcripts go to a private store, are readable only by the advisor who held the conversation, and expire after a day.
  • Sources are built from what the tools returned, so the AI cannot invent a citation. There are no binding quotes, approvals or claim payouts.
  • One shared guardrail covers content, personal data and denied topics such as legal and tax advice. Policies and notes are saved only when the advisor asks or confirms.
Appendix

End-to-end on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore

Models: Claude Haiku 5.5 (chat, comparator, recommendations, document and brochure reading) · Nova 2.5 Sonic (voice). One shared Bedrock Guardrail gates the six Claude surfaces; voice is not covered. Before every tool call, the gateway checks the advisor's own sign-in token. Five CDK stacks; every AgentCore resource is stable CloudFormation.

  • The React + Vite app runs on CloudFront and holds only a Cognito JWT, no IAM credentials.
  • The text runtime uses Strands with Claude Haiku 5.5. The voice runtime uses Strands BidiAgent with Nova 2.5 Sonic over WebSocket.
  • The AgentCore Gateway offers 12 targets and 20 tools over MCP, and its request interceptor checks the advisor's token before every tool call. Memory is per customer with a 90-day TTL.
  • One shared Bedrock Guardrail covers the six Claude surfaces, not voice. Voice traces carry timings and tool names, not conversation content. Five CDK stacks.
