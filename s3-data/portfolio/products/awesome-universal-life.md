# Awesome Universal Life
### Type: Universal Life Insurance
**Awesome Universal Life Insurance** provides permanent coverage with a no-lapse guarantee and flexible premiums. The death benefit is guaranteed to the age the customer chooses (90, 95, 100 or lifetime), as long as at least the required no-lapse premium is paid on time. Above that minimum, the customer can pay the target premium or overfund within IRS limits, and the policy's cash value earns interest at a declared rate with a guaranteed minimum floor. Unlike whole life, Awesome Universal Life separates the insurance cost from the cash value accumulation, giving policyholders visibility into what they are paying for protection and what is building as equity. Best suited for customers who want a permanent death benefit they can rely on, with room to adjust contributions to match life circumstances: business owners with uneven income, professionals planning for career transitions, and families planning an estate.
### Customer-Selectable Features
1. **Death Benefit** - Select from $250,000, $500,000, $1,000,000, $2,000,000, up to $5,000,000 permanent coverage.
2. **Death Benefit Option** - Choose Option A (level death benefit, where the benefit stays constant as cash value grows) or Option B (increasing death benefit equal to the face amount plus accumulated cash value).
3. **No-Lapse Guarantee Age** - Choose how long the death benefit is guaranteed: to age 90, 95, 100 or lifetime (age 121). A longer guarantee has a higher required premium.
4. **Required No-Lapse Premium** - The least that must be paid each year to keep the guarantee to the chosen age. Every premium estimate shows it. Paying less, or paying late, can reduce or permanently end the guarantee even while there is cash value, unless the No-Lapse Guarantee Rider is added.
5. **Premium Flexibility** - Pay the required no-lapse premium, the target premium, or overfund up to IRS-defined limits to accelerate cash value growth.
6. **Premium Payment Frequency** - Options for monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, or annual premium payments.
7. **Interest Crediting** - Cash value earns a declared interest rate reset periodically, subject to a contractually guaranteed minimum floor (typically 2% or 3%).
8. **Death Benefit Adjustment** - Ability to increase the death benefit with new underwriting, or decrease it without underwriting, as family and financial circumstances change.
9. **1035 Exchange** - Cash value from an existing life policy or annuity can be moved into Awesome Universal Life tax-free under Section 1035.
### Optional Coverage (riders)
1. **Waiver of Premium** - Waives the monthly deduction and policy charges if the policyholder becomes totally disabled, preserving cash value and the guarantee through the disability period.
2. **Accidental Death Benefit** - Pays an additional death benefit if death results from an accident.
3. **Long-Term Care / Chronic Illness** - Allows the policyholder to accelerate a portion of the death benefit to cover qualifying long-term care expenses or a chronic condition, or on a terminal diagnosis.
4. **Guaranteed Purchase Option** - Allows the policyholder to buy additional cover at set future dates without new medical evidence.
5. **No-Lapse Guarantee Rider** - Lets a late or missed premium be caught up within 12 months without losing the no-lapse guarantee.
6. **Overloan Protection** - Stops the policy from lapsing when policy loans grow larger than the cash value.
### Application
The application asks about the proposed insured, the owner and payor if they are different people, up to three beneficiaries, the chosen riders and premium, existing life insurance and any replacement, and a medical and lifestyle history. The customer receives an illustration showing the required no-lapse premium before signing, and acknowledges it on the signed application.
