## Riders
- **Future Increase Option:** raise the monthly benefit by up to $1,500
  every 3 years without new medical underwriting, subject to income proof
- **Catastrophic Disability Rider:** additional $2,000 per month if the
  insured cannot perform two or more activities of daily living
- **Return of Premium on Survival Rider:** not elected
- **Waiver of Premium:** premiums waived after 90 days of covered disability
## Exclusions

## Notes
This policy replaces up to 57% of the insured's gross earned income if he
is unable to work because of a covered sickness or injury. It supplements
and does not coordinate with any employer group disability plan. Benefits
are paid monthly in arrears. The policy includes a 30-day right-to-examine
period.
